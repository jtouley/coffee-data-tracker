> Two changes cause these:
> 
> - **`set(stated + regions) - {c}` → `set(stated) - {c}`** in `origin_country`. A title country that is a variety's name now wins whenever the real origin is given only as a region. That is the El Puente class #41 was built to stop.
>   - RESULTS still says "Without that country it gives None, never Kenya". That is now false as soon as the title carries "Kenya".
>   - `test_el_puente_lots` misses it only because its title has no country.
>   - The `ABSTAIN` comment in `tests/test_extract.py` ("a region elsewhere disagrees") is now stale.
> - **Un-blanking "Colombia variety/varietal/cultivar"**. Combined with the change above, a title or body that mentions the Colombia variety now outranks the place the coffee actually grew. That flips right answers to wrong ones (the three rows where main is right).
> 
> Neither change has measured support. Every v2→v3 difference on train and validation comes from the label-field lookahead, not from these two rules. I checked with an alternative that:
> 
> 1. keeps the region veto unless the text also names a region of the title country, so the PR's "lot from Nyeri" case still gives Kenya;
> 2. keeps blanking "Colombia variety", except before a ":" or "Series".

> It gives **exactly the branch's predictions on all 35,880 train and validation products**. It passes `test_region_only_where_coffee_grew`, `test_colombia_varietal_is_still_colombia` and `test_el_puente_lots`. It turns every row above into the right answer or None.
