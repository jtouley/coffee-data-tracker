# Wholesale listing: labeling definition

A listing is **wholesale** when it is offered to business buyers rather than to someone buying coffee for
themselves: it says wholesale, B2B, foodservice, resale or private label; it belongs to a wholesale/B2B
catalog (product type or a tag that marks the listing itself as wholesale-only); or it is a case of retail
bags, or portion/fraction packs and in-room/office packs sold by the case for commercial brewers.

It is **retail** otherwise. A large bag (2 lb, 5 lb) a person can buy for themselves is retail. A retail
listing tagged as *also* available to wholesale accounts is retail. Equipment, green coffee and merch are
judged the same way (they are a classifier question, not a wholesale one).

**Unclear**: the title, type, tags and sizes don't say which (e.g. a roaster that tags some retail-looking
coffees "Wholesale" without any other sign). Unclear items are reported separately, not counted either way.
