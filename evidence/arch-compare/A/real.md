# Design A, real crawl of every Shopify roaster (2026-10-31 15:16-16:12 UTC, this environment's pooled egress)
**Setup.** Worktree A/wt (branch arch-a, local). All 1,070 ingestable Shopify roasters in the registry seed (1,058 hosts), run like `weekly`: ingest.run (per-store backoff, 2 deferred rounds, refused labelling, 170-min deadline), then the --retry-failed pass. Publishing off, checked before the first request: HF_TOKEN and HUGGING_FACE_HUB_TOKEN unset (no cached token file), huggingface_hub.HfApi and upload/download functions stubbed to raise (checked), lake local (A/real/lake), Lake.publish a no-op, HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1. Politeness unchanged: robots.txt, the crawler's User-Agent, per-host spacing, 3 req/s Shopify, 32 workers. Hard cap 9,000 requests (not reached). Every response is in A/real_responses.jsonl (ts, host, path, status, Retry-After; limiter refusals that were never sent are flagged by `error`). Script: A/real/real_crawl.py; metrics: A/real/analyze_real.py -> A/real/metrics.json.

**Stores.** 1,070 attempted: 1,034 ok = 96.6% [95.4, 97.6] (1,026 with products); 24 refused (labelled `refused:` in fetches); 14 other failures (13 FetchError, 1 Blocked). Main pass parked 407 stores, 58 still failing after round 1, 45 after round 2; the retry pass got 47 and left 34.
**429 pattern.** Every Retry-After was 60. 392 of 1,058 hosts (37% [34, 40]) got a 429; 379 of them later answered 200, so most 429s were temporary per-store pauses that a 60 s wait cleared. 217 of 1,044 hosts (21%) got a 429 on their first catalog request, at a steady 18-24% in each 5-minute block while the crawl ran at 3.0 req/s (minutes 0-15); there was no build-up or burst, as a shared IP bucket would show. robots.txt never got a 429 (0 of 1,123), and there were no 430s (Shopify's IP-wide code). A hard core persists: 11 of the 13 stores that refused in the 01:44 live test got 429s again, and 24 stores refused every attempt.
**Verdict: per-store, with a caveat.** The evidence points to per-store limits: per-host Retry-After, per-host recovery, no 430s, and a first-contact rate that stays flat at full speed. But the share of stores hit (37%) is far above the live test's 5-8% from this egress 13 h earlier, so some pooled-egress (shared IP) effect can't be ruled out.
**Prototype weakness seen live.** Deferred-round retries tripped the burst detector 5 times ("5 of 8 stores blocked within 30s: platform-wide"). Each trip paused all Shopify for 60 s and cut the rate to 0.38 req/s for the rest of the run, so the deferred rounds and the retry pass took 20 of the 56 minutes at about 0.3 req/s. This is the bug live.md already reported; it is not fixed in #62's branch.
