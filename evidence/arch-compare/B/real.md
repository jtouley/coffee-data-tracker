# Design B, real Shopify connector job: all 1,070 Shopify roasters (2026-10-31 19:21:41-20:05:47 UTC, this container's pooled egress)
**Setup.** The code was a frozen copy of B/wt (B/real/frozen): origin/main 6bda240 + PR #62 + PAGINATION.patch + PAGINATION.v2.patch (conditional short-page stop), taken before the ELT change and before the parked-`body_text` fix. The run was `connectors.crawl(lake, "shopify", ...)`: 32 workers, 3 req/s, page backoffs of 60/120/240 s with no worker held, parking after 3 backoffs, and a 225-min deadline. The separate retry job (`crawl-retry`) followed for the parked stores. Publishing was off: HF_TOKEN and HUGGING_FACE_HUB_TOKEN were unset, huggingface_hub was stubbed to raise (checked before start), and the lake was local (B/real/lake). Politeness was unchanged: robots.txt, the crawler's User-Agent and per-host spacing. I held real.lock ("B 2026-10-31T19:21:41Z") after it had been free for 20 minutes, then released it. Every response is in B/real_responses.jsonl (time, host, path, page, status, Retry-After, and the job phase). Metrics are in B/real/summary.json (script B/real/analyze_real.py).
**Time.** The main job took 26.0 min (1,561 s) and crawl-retry for the 58 parked stores took 18.1 min, so 44 min in all: **well inside 240 min**, with nothing skipped at the deadline. The process segfaulted at exit (code 139) after it had written retry_out.json and compacted the partition. No data was lost.
**Requests.** 4,101 requests went out (3,816 in the main job, 285 in the retry job). The limiter also held back another 837 attempts, which are not counted. Responses: 3,239 × 200, 816 × 429, **0 × 430**, 17 × 503, and 26 redirects. Every Retry-After was 60.
**Stores.** **1,030 of 1,070 ok = 96.3% [94.9, 97.2]**, and 1,021 of those returned products. 40 failed: 20 `refused` (429 on page 1 through all 3 backoffs in both jobs), 18 `parked` (blocked on a later page), 1 Blocked and 1 other FetchError. A got 96.6% [95.4, 97.6]: the same within the intervals.
**429 pattern.** It looks per-store, not IP-wide, as in A's run. 371 of 1,058 hosts (35% [32, 38]) got a 429, and 230 of 1,044 (22% [20, 25]) got one on their first /products.json. Per 10-minute block the 429s fell from 221 to 319 to 131 to 105 to 40 as the crawl moved from new stores to backoff retries. They came from 220-231 distinct hosts per block, not as a burst. After a 429, the same host's next request was a 200 415 times and another 429 352 times. 0 platform-wide pauses in the main job; 7 in the retry job, where only blockers are asked.
**The 11 page-2 stores: 4 now succeed** (Andytown, Death Wish, Red Bay, Winans). Andytown and Winans were parked on page 2 and recovered in crawl-retry, which resumed at page 2. Counter Culture, Colectivo and Coffee AM were served page 1 but parked on page 2. This fresh local lake had no product history, so the conditional short-page stop couldn't skip their empty page 2. With last week's count in a real lake they should end at page 1 (likely 3 more). Cat & Cloud, Cuvée, Four Barrel and Groundwork refused page 1 itself this time.
**The 22 stores A refused: 8 now succeed, and 5 refused again** (the other 9 failed differently, mostly parked).
**Caveats.** This is one run from this container's pooled egress, 1-4 h after A's and B's earlier crawls of the same stores, so per-store windows may still have been open. The frozen code predates the parked-`body_text` fix, which only touches the bodies rows of the 58 stores that went through crawl-retry.
