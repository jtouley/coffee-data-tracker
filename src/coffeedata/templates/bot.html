In short: RegistryCoffeeBot reads US coffee roasters' public product catalogs once a week for registry.coffee. It follows robots.txt and spaces its requests to each store. To stop it, add a robots.txt rule or email us.

Who we are

registry.coffee is a free, personal project run by one person: a weekly catalog of the coffee US roasters sell. The crawler identifies itself as:

{{ user_agent }}

Before the name changed, it identified itself as coffee-data. Rules for either name still apply.

What we crawl

  • Public product catalogs only, such as a shop's /products.json feed or its product pages: names, prices, sizes and stock, the facts a shopper can see.
  • Once a week, on Mondays.
  • Never a login, checkout, account or anything behind a password or a challenge.

How we crawl

  • We read your robots.txt first, and don't fetch anything it disallows. If it answers with a server error, or not at all, we don't crawl until it answers.
  • Requests to one store are spaced at least 1 second apart.
  • We honour a robots.txt Crawl-delay up to 60 seconds between requests. If you want slower crawling than that, email {{ contact }}.
  • We honour Retry-After, and stop and come back later when a store answers 429 or 503.

How to opt out

  • Add a rule to your robots.txt. We read it at the start of every weekly crawl: 
    User-agent: RegistryCoffeeBot
Disallow: /
  • Or email {{ contact }}, or use the "Add a roaster" form and tick "Opt out". An opted-out store is never fetched again, and its coffees drop out of every public table.
