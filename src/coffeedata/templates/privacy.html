In short: the site and the app count visits, searches and clicks to roasters without cookies or an ID, and never keep your full IP address. The invite-only app keeps what you log so it can work. Who runs this

registry.coffee is run by an individual (the "operator", "we" or "us"), not a company. This policy covers registry.coffee (the launch page and the catalog), the shot-logging app at app.registry.coffee (the "app"), and the public dataset built from the catalog.

The public site and catalog

  • The site doesn't set cookies, or load scripts, fonts or images from other services. It counts how it is used, as described under What we count.
  • If you switch between light and dark, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may log them for security. That's covered by GitHub's privacy statement; we don't receive those logs.
  • Catalog links take you to roasters' own websites, whose privacy policies apply there.
  • The catalog lists products and businesses, not people. It holds what roasters publish on their public storefronts: coffee names, prices, stock and the like.

What we count

We count page visits, searches, filters and clicks to roasters, on the site and in the app. We do it without cookies, without storing anything in your browser, without an ID, and never tied to your account. For each one we keep:

  • What you searched for, exactly as you typed it. Don't type anything private into the search box.
  • The filters and sort order you chose, and how many results there were.
  • Which roaster or coffee link you clicked, and its position in the results.
  • Which page you were on, whether you were on a phone or a computer, and which weekly build of the catalog it showed.
  • The site or campaign that sent you: only the other site's name, never the page.
  • The hour we received it, not the minute.
  • A shortened IP address (for example 203.0.113.x), to work out your country, state and city. We never store your full IP address: we shorten it straight away. The city is often your internet provider's nearby city rather than where you are.

We check your browser type only to mark visits from bots, and don't keep it.

Our reports show only weekly totals. A search or place appears in one only once it has turned up on several days, and small towns are never named.

Links to roasters carry utm_source=registry.coffee and utm_medium=referral, so a roaster can see the visit came from us. With Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track on, nothing is counted.

IP location data by DB-IP, city populations by GeoNames (both CC BY 4.0).

What the app collects

The app is invite-only. When you use it, we keep:

  • Your account: your username and a hash of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • Your email address, if you add one: used for account recovery and, unless you untick the box, for marketing. It's stored encrypted, so only the app can read it, and it's never used to sign in. Besides you, the operator can see it, to help you back into your account, and Resend receives it to deliver each email.
  • Your choices: whether you agreed to the Terms, whether you get marketing email, and whether your shots and bags may be counted in insights for roasters (below), and when you chose each.
  • What you log: shots, bags, equipment and any notes you write.
  • Bag photos you upload. Location and other EXIF metadata is removed before the photo is stored.
  • Technical data:
    • Your IP address, which the app holds in memory only, to apply rate limits.
    • App logs of each request's route, status, timing and your user id. Request contents aren't logged.
    • Request logs that Google Cloud records automatically for every request: IP address, browser details, the URL requested and the response status. They're kept in Google Cloud Logging for about 30 days by default.
  • Cookies: five, none used for tracking or advertising:
    • session keeps you signed in;
    • theme remembers light, dark or automatic;
    • hand remembers which side the − and + buttons go on;
    • tz holds your phone's time zone, so times show in your local time;
    • nudge-email remembers that you chose "Not now" when the app suggested adding an email, for 90 days.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: show your history, suggest your next shot, and match bags to the catalog.
  • To recommend coffees, from the bags, ratings and shots you log, matched against the public catalog. This happens inside the app.
  • To email you about your account, if you add an email: a link to confirm it, a link to reset your password when you ask for one, and a notice when your password changes.
  • To email you occasional news about new coffees and features, unless you untick that box when you sign up. You can change it in Setup at any time.
  • To keep it secure and prevent abuse, for example with rate limits.
  • To find and fix problems.
  • To see which searches, filters and roasters people use on the site and in the app, and roughly where they come from (the counts above).

We may also use aggregated or de-identified data, which can't reasonably identify you, to run and improve the service and to publish statistics about it. For example: "washed Ethiopians are most often brewed at 1:2.2".

Insights for roasters. We may in future sell roasters reports built from the shots and bags logged across many members: for example, how a coffee tends to be dialed in, or which origins members brew most. No report has been sold yet. Before we release any report, we will make sure that:

  • it contains only totals and averages, and never names, usernames, email addresses, photos, notes or any single shot;
  • each figure comes from at least 11 members, and any figure that could be worked out from the others is hidden;
  • we keep a record of every report we release;
  • the roaster has agreed in writing not to try to identify anyone from it. We won't try either.

Your personal data isn't sold. If you'd rather your shots and bags weren't counted, turn off Include my shots and bags in roaster insights in Setup → Account, and any later report will leave them out.

Who processes it

We use these services to run registry.coffee. Each receives only what it needs for its part:

  • Hugging Face: stores the app's data, the counts above (privately) and the public dataset.
  • Resend: sends the app's emails.
  • Google Cloud: hosts the app and the counter that receives the counts above (Cloud Run, in the United States) and keeps the app's logs.
  • Anthropic: reads the label on a bag photo, and only when you upload one, to fill in the coffee's details.
  • GitHub: hosts the public site and the code.

Your data is stored and processed in the United States.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use third-party tracking or analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. The data store is append-only, meaning records are added rather than overwritten. So when you ask for deletion, we remove your records within 30 days. Copies may remain in backups or in the data store's version history for a limited time afterwards, until they're overwritten or expire.

Your email address is different: when you remove or change it, or your account is deleted, every copy of it is deleted from the data store, including its version history.

The counts are different too: we keep them permanently, searches and shortened IP addresses included, in the same append-only store. Nothing links them to you, so we can't find or delete yours on request.

Your choices and rights

Email {{ contact }} and tell us your username (writing from the email address on your account, if you added one, helps us confirm it's you) to:

  • get a copy of your data
  • correct it
  • delete it, along with your account

We may need to confirm it's you before acting. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law, and we'll honor them where they apply.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS everywhere
  • password hashing
  • email addresses encrypted
  • secrets kept out of the code
  • access limited to what each part needs

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee that yours won't be accessed, disclosed or lost.

Children

registry.coffee isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes
