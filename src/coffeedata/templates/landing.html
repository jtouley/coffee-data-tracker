Query it from anywhere

No account, no download. DuckDB, Polars and pandas read the Parquet straight from Hugging Face. Every coffee links back to the roaster that sells it.

  1. FoundAbout 3,200 roasters from Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap, plus additions from the community.
  2. ReadEach public storefront feed is read once a week, politely, following robots.txt.
  3. UnderstoodRules, with Claude for what they miss, pull out origin, process, varietal, elevation and notes.
  4. Publisheddbt builds the history and the public tables are pushed to Hugging Face.
-- duckdb: cheapest washed Ethiopians in stock
select roaster_name, title, price_per_100g, url
from
  '{{ links.hf_path or "hf://datasets/<owner>/<name>/data" }}/public_coffee.parquet'
where origin_country = 'Ethiopia'
  and process = 'washed' and in_stock
order by price_per_100g
limit 10;
