# Is this place an actual roaster that sells coffee it roasts, online?

The answer is `roaster_online` only when all three of these hold:

- **It roasts coffee itself.** The business, or the company that owns it under the same brand, roasts the beans. A
  roaster's own flagship café counts.
- **It sells that coffee by the bag**, whole bean or ground, by weight, under its own name. Samplers, pods and drinks
  don't count.
- **Online.** A person can put a bag in a cart on this site, or on a shop it links as its own under the same brand.
  Phone orders, in-person sales and marketplaces don't count.

Answer `not_roaster` when the evidence shows the place is something else: a café that serves or resells another
roaster's coffee (bags under another roaster's name, "proudly serving ..."), a reseller or private label, a store
with no coffee for sale by the bag, a closed or parked site, a business that isn't about coffee, or a chain's
corporate site.

Answer `unsure` when the evidence can't tell, for example when the page shows bags under the place's own name but
nothing says who roasts them, or when there is too little text or too few products to judge. Unsure is a good answer:
a person reviews every place you are unsure about. Never guess.

Judge only from the evidence given: the place's name and domain, its listing's tags, its home page's text, and the
first products of its online store. Product descriptions copied from a roaster prove nothing about who roasts. Give
one short sentence of reason that names the evidence you relied on.
